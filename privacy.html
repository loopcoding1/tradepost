Privacy Policy for CardDuel

Publisher: TRADEPOST TCG LTD. Effective Date: October 2026 Application: CardDuel (Android)

Quick Summary

CardDuel is an offline card duel game. We do not collect, transmit, share, or sell any personal data. There are no user accounts, no ads, no trackers, and no external analytics.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

CardDuel operates entirely offline on your device. We respect your fundamental right to privacy:

  • No Personal Identifiable Information: We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, or physical address.
  • No Account Required: You do not need to register, log in, or provide credentials to use the application.
  • No Location Data: We do not request, access, or track your GPS or network location.
  • No Financial Data: The application does not process, store, or transmit payment or financial data.

2. Device Storage & Local Data

All game data (such as game state, player decks, scores, and preferences) is stored strictly locally on your device in application-private storage.

  • This data remains on your phone and is never sent to our servers or any third-party cloud.
  • Android cloud backup is disabled to ensure your data stays exclusively on your hardware.

3. Advertising & Third-Party Services

We do not partner with third-party advertising networks or analytics SDKs:

  • No Advertisements: CardDuel contains zero third-party banner, interstitial, or rewarded ads.
  • No Trackers or Analytics: We do not use Google Analytics, Firebase, or any tracking SDKs.

4. Permissions

CardDuel requires no sensitive device permissions (such as Camera, Microphone, Contacts, or Precise Location). It only runs with standard base Android application permissions necessary to execute the game engine.

5. Data Erasure & Control

You have total control over your local data at all times:

  • You can erase your local data directly within the game via About & Privacy → Erase Data.
  • You can clear application data through your device's Settings → Apps → CardDuel → Clear Storage.
  • Uninstalling CardDuel permanently and automatically removes all associated local game files from your device.

6. Children's Privacy

CardDuel does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The application complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families Policies.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Any updates will be posted to this page with a revised "Effective Date". Because we do not collect contact information, we encourage users to review this page periodically.
